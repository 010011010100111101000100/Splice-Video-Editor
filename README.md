# Splice – browser video editor

Everything runs in the visitor's browser. Nothing is uploaded, saved, or tied to an account.

## Using it
- Drop in video, audio, or images, then click **Add to video** or **Add as audio**.
- Drag the clip edges to trim. Drag audio clips to move them.
- **Cut at playhead** (or press S) splits a clip. Delete removes it.
- Select a clip to change volume, speed, fades, text, and colour.
- **Export video** records the timeline in real time and downloads it. Keep the tab open while it runs.

Works best in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox on a computer.

## Also included
- **Text & elements:** add titles, shapes and emoji stickers on their own timeline row. Change colour, size, position, rotation and entrance.
- **Music library and sound effects:** four music tracks and four effects, created in your browser when you click them (no downloads, no copyright issues).
- **Filters** (black and white, vintage, warm, cool, dreamy blur), **undo/redo** (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Y), **fullscreen** (F or double-click the preview), **save frame** as PNG, and arrow keys to step through the video.

## Studio features
- **Movie and Short modes** in the header. Short switches to a vertical 9:16 frame with a dashed guide for YouTube's on-screen buttons. Movie has optional cinema bars.
- **Instruments** button: grand piano, electric piano, acoustic guitar, bass, violin section, flute, marimba, synth lead and a drum kit. Play with the mouse, touch, or your keyboard, press Record, and your performance is added to the audio bar.
- **Custom bars:** add extra text or audio bars, click a bar name to put new items on it, double-click to rename.
- Twelve more sound effects.
